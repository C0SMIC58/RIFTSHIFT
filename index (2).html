WHY RIFTSHIFT

RUNS THAT KEEP CHANGING.

The game is built around repeatable runs rather than a fixed campaign. Your arena, objectives, upgrades, contracts, events and boss pressure change as you progress.

⌁

Procedural Arenas

Cover, stairs, elevated decks, rift hazards, vents, cables, lighting and randomized arena layouts.

▲

Weapon Evolution

Stack upgrades and Legendaries, then evolve each class into specialized late-run builds.

✦

Rift Contracts

Opt into dangerous challenge rules for extra Scrap, rerolls and guaranteed Epic rewards.

!

Boss Modifiers

Late-run bosses can become Frenzied, Armored, Mirrored, Vampiric or Overloaded.

☇

Energy Abilities

Every class has a distinct Q ability powered by a rechargeable Energy resource.

∞

Endless Pressure

Mini-bosses, rare arena events, objectives and escalating wave rules keep long runs dangerous.
