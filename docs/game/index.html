YOUR RUN. YOUR LOADOUT.

Deploy into unstable Rift zones, complete objectives under pressure, evolve your class kit, and survive long enough to see what the Director sends next.

Run Loop

HOW A SHIFT UNFOLDS

The structure is simple. Surviving it is not.

DEPLOY

Enter a biome with a class, starting arsenal, and difficulty protocol.

SURVIVE THE WAVE

Fight standard enemies, specialists, elites, roaming threats, and faction pressure.

COMPLETE OBJECTIVES

Escort drones, sync stabilizers, follow safe zones, transport unstable cores, and respond to Rift Fork choices.

EVOLVE

Choose upgrades, branch secondary/melee/utility gear, collect artifacts, and decide whether a curse is worth the power.

FACE THE BOSS

Boss encounters, rematches, secret threats, and escalating run depth test the build you created.
