The first stable transit record was not a victory report. It was a warning: the Rift does not open into one place. It opens into pressure—different worlds, different forces, all trying to occupy the same coordinates.
Blacksite security doctrine treated the Rift as an intrusion event. The problem was simple: after enough shifts, nobody could agree which side of the containment line was the intrusion.
Some hostiles are not invaders in the usual sense. Riftborn signatures suggest organisms or soldiers shaped by repeated exposure to unstable transitions.
The C-11 program focused on channeling Rift energy through a weapon platform before it could destabilize the user. The resulting rail lance is powerful, temperamental, and still classified as an active hazard.
Contracts were meant to make dangerous deployments measurable. Complete the objective, get paid, go deeper. Eventually the contracts stopped asking whether going deeper was a good idea.
Beyond the standard operational depth is a signal that should not exist. Internal records refer to it with one phrase: THE BREACH.
